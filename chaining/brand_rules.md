# Brand Rules – PocketStudio Creative Pipeline

## Objective
Generate clear, truthful and audience-focused advertising content.

## Mandatory Rules
1. Never use unsupported product claims.
2. Do not use superlatives such as:
   - Best
   - Cheapest
   - Number one
   - Guaranteed
3. Do not invent prices, discounts or product specifications.
4. Use only the features provided in the product brief.
5. Keep the advertising message simple and engaging.
6. Match the language and tone to the target audience.
7. Every advertisement must include a clear call to action.
8. Reject misleading or exaggerated statements.

## Selection Criteria
- Brand compliance
- Accuracy of product information
- Audience relevance
- Clarity of headline and body
- Quality of call to action

## Final Decision
Select the strongest variant that follows all mandatory rules.
